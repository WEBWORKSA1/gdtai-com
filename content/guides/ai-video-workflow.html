AI can now help at every stage of video production. The creators who win are not the ones who automate everything — platforms increasingly reward originality — but the ones who use AI to remove drudgery so they can spend more time on ideas, research and personality. Here is a practical end-to-end workflow.

1. Idea and angle

Use an assistant to brainstorm 30 angles on your topic, then pick the one where you have a genuine opinion or experience. Ask for the questions your audience is likely asking and the misconceptions they hold. A distinctive angle matters more than any tool below.

2. Research and script

Gather sources yourself and paste the key material into an assistant. Ask for an outline with a strong hook in the first few seconds, a clear structure and a payoff. Then write or heavily edit the script in your own voice. Verify every fact and number before recording.

3. Voice

Recording your own voice builds trust and is usually best. If you need a voiceover, text-to-speech tools like ElevenLabs or Murf produce natural results. Clean up home recordings with Adobe Podcast's speech enhancement or Krisp for noise removal.

4. Visuals

  • B-roll and scenes: generative video tools such as Runway, Luma, Kling or Pika can create short shots; use them for illustration, not as a substitute for substance.
  • Images and thumbnails: Midjourney, Ideogram or Adobe Firefly for concepts; Ideogram is particularly good when you need legible text.
  • Avatars: Synthesia or HeyGen for training content and translations, disclosed clearly to viewers.

5. Edit

Descript lets you edit video by editing the transcript — delete a sentence and the clip is cut. CapCut and VEED add automatic captions, templates and effects quickly. Keep pacing tight: cut pauses, add pattern interrupts every few seconds in shorts.

6. Captions, title and thumbnail

Auto-captions improve retention and accessibility; always proof-read names and numbers. Generate ten title options, then pick one that is specific and honest. Test two thumbnail concepts with high contrast and few words.

7. Repurpose

Tools like Opus Clip find highlight moments in long videos and produce vertical shorts with captions. Turn the script into a blog post and newsletter section so one piece of work feeds every channel.

Stay on the right side of platform rules

  • Disclose realistic synthetic or altered content where platforms require it.
  • Do not mass-produce repetitive, low-effort videos; add commentary, analysis and original value.
  • Only use music, footage and voices you have rights to use.
