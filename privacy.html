Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information GDTai ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide: when you submit a form (newsletter, AI Stack Match, contact, tool submission, donation pledge, contest entry or job application) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address and message.
  • Usage and device data: like most websites, our hosting provider and advertising partners may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and referring pages.
  • Local preferences: we store settings such as theme, saved tools and cookie choices in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device.

How we use information

  • To respond to your requests and send what you asked for (for example, your AI stack recommendations or the newsletter).
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To show advertising that keeps the site free.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We share AI Stack Match details with an implementation partner only if you explicitly request partner quotes.

Advertising and cookies (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland may be shown a consent message so they can choose whether to allow personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Form processing

Forms on this site are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-forwarding service (FormSubmit). The information you enter is transmitted securely over HTTPS to that service solely to deliver your message to us.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube using privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and load only after you click play. Tool logos are loaded from Google's public favicon service. These providers may collect data according to their own policies.

Data retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable record-keeping, then delete them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's DPDP Act or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request".

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions? Reach us through the contact form.
